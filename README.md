### ☀️  Intelligent Hybrid Renewable Microgrid
A sustainable energy solution designed to provide reliable, affordable, and clean electricity to remote tribal villages where conventional grid extension is difficult and expensive.

The project explores an intelligent hybrid microgrid combining solar energy, battery storage, and biomass/biogas backup to support essential community services.

**Key Features:**
- 30–35 kWp Solar PV System
- 120–150 kWh LiFePO₄ Battery Energy Storage
- 25 kW Hybrid Inverter
- 10 kW Biomass/Biogas Backup System
- Intelligent Energy Management System (EMS)
- Priority-based load management
- Predictive energy management
- Community energy sharing
- IoT-based monitoring and control
- Modular and scalable microgrid architecture

**Technologies:** Solar PV, ESP32, IoT, Renewable Energy Systems, React, Vite, Tailwind CSS, MQTT

**Goal:** Build a sustainable and intelligent energy ecosystem that improves access to electricity for healthcare, education, water supply, households, and community development.
